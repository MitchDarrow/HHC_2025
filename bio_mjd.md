## Mitch Darrow

**Manager, Government Assurance Practice Group**

**Office:** Portland, ME

**Education:** Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering, Iowa State University

**Started with BerryDunn:** 2014


Mitch is a manager BerryDunn’s Government Assurance Practice Group and leads BerryDunn’s vulnerability scanning and penetration testing services. With 25 years of experience in business system analysis, database design, system architecture, network administration, incident response, and design engineering. He also provides leadership on security engagements to measure, analyze, and improve performance issues, training and development, project coordination, and strategy and planning. 

**Certifications:** GIAC Penetration Tester (GPEN)

**Interests:** Mitch is a registered Maine Kayak Guide and can be found paddling the Maine Coast and Great Lakes when he is not doing cybersecurity work. Mitch is a member of several running groups in the community and loves running on the Maine trails when they are not snowbound. Besides these things, Mitch is known to take on seemingly impossible problems. He is currently renovating a cabin built in 1896 in the Adirondack Mountains of New York State. When he isn't jacking the cabin, breaking granite, or rebuilding walls he can be found restoring a 20' 1954 Chris-craft Riviera.|
